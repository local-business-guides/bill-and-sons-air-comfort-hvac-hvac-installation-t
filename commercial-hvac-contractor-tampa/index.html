What the work covers and key facts

Commercial HVAC service can include inspection, troubleshooting, repair, or replacement, depending on the building and the issue. A provider should first understand the property and system, then describe what they found and what steps they recommend. The exact process depends on the equipment and the job.

Key facts to discuss:

  • Coverage: Confirm the contractor works on your building type and specific HVAC system.
  • Timing: Ask when an assessment can happen and how equipment access, the size of the job, or parts availability may affect the schedule.
  • Cost factors: The system's condition, the work required, equipment needs, and access can affect cost. Request an estimate for your own situation rather than relying on a general figure.
  • Terms: “Rooftop unit” means HVAC equipment installed on a roof. “Load” refers to the heating or cooling demand a system is expected to handle. “Troubleshooting” is the process of checking a system to find the cause of a problem.
